Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MeriHoor application and automation tools ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Scope and Purpose

MeriHoor is an internal content management and automation software created exclusively for managing video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, playlists), scheduling, and publishing workflows on the official MeriHoor YouTube channel.

2. Information Collection and Google User Data

Our application interacts with the Google YouTube Data API. We only request the minimum permissions required to perform authorized channel management tasks:

  • YouTube Data Access: To retrieve video details, upload video files, update metadata (titles, descriptions, tags), and configure scheduled release times.
  • Authentication Credentials: OAuth tokens are securely stored locally and used solely to authenticate API requests with Google servers.

We do not collect, sell, transfer, or share personal user data with any third parties.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

MeriHoor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are stored securely in local configuration files. No external databases or third-party servers store your authentication or account credentials.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke access to your Google account permissions at any time through your Google Security Settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
